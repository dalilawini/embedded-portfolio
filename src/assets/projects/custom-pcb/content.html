REQUIREMENTS

From circuit need to manufacturable board

This project covers the practical chain from an embedded circuit requirement to a fabricated, assembled, and tested custom PCB.

SCHEMATIC & LAYOUT

Design before fabrication

Altium Designer is used to capture the schematic and develop the board layout. Design choices are considered alongside routing, component placement, and later assembly needs.

MANUFACTURING WORKFLOW

CAM to CNC

  1. Prepare the PCB layout and manufacturing outputs.
  2. Use FlatCAM to prepare toolpaths.
  3. Machine the board on a CNC 3018 Pro.
  4. Assemble components and verify the board during bring-up.

ASSEMBLY & TESTING

Validate one stage at a time

Assembly and test close the loop between design and hardware. Checking each stage before moving forward helps isolate fabrication, soldering, and bring-up issues.

LESSONS LEARNED

Design for the real workflow

CNC tolerances, toolpaths, and assembly constraints need to influence the PCB design early. That makes the workflow more repeatable for later prototypes.
