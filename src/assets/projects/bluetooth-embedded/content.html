SCOPE

Practical wireless embedded exploration

This collection brings together experiments with ESP32 Bluetooth, Bluetooth HID, nRF52832, HM-10 / CC2541 modules, and RF communication.

PROTOCOL CHOICE

Match the link to the interaction

Wireless protocol selection is approached through the practical needs of each prototype: power use, range, reliability, and the expected interaction with a host or peripheral.

BLUETOOTH HID

Embedded peripherals and hosts

Bluetooth HID experiments investigate how an embedded device can participate in familiar peripheral-style interactions.

INTEROPERABILITY

Connection behavior matters

Connection reliability and device interoperability are central concerns when evaluating Bluetooth and RF modules across different embedded targets.

OUTCOME

A broader wireless toolkit

The work provides hands-on experience across several wireless technologies and a clearer basis for choosing an approach in future embedded projects.
